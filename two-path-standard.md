# Two-Path Standard

A task becomes eligible for review through exactly one of these paths.

## Path 1: Complete

The requested result is finished and verified against its acceptance criteria.

A completion report should state:

- what changed;
- what was verified;
- the final result or artifact; and
- anything intentionally left out of scope.

Starting, planning, attempting, or producing an unverified partial result is not completion.

## Path 2: Exact handoff

The task cannot be completed without a human action that the system cannot perform.

The blocker report must state:

- the exact blocking condition;
- the exact action the reviewer must take;
- where to take it; and
- what work resumes after that action.

“Need access,” “something failed,” or a long technical explanation without a concrete next action does not meet this path.

## Review test

Ask two questions:

1. Is the requested outcome fully complete and verified?
2. If not, was the reviewer told exactly what to do next?

If both answers are no, the task is not eligible for a cookie.
