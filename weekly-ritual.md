# Weekly Ritual

## Monday tally and reset

At the start of each Monday in Pacific Time:

1. Close the previous week.
2. Count explicit awards and subtract explicit revocations.
3. Preserve the final ledger and decision references.
4. Record the final total without changing any decision.
5. Open a new week with a total of zero.
6. Clear the prior week’s thirst-signal marker.
7. Confirm the hourly checker is scheduled and healthy.

A reset starts a new counting window; it must not delete history.

## During the week

- Record only explicit award, denial, defer, and take-back decisions.
- Keep task states current.
- Run the pace check every hour.
- Stay silent when on pace.
- When behind, send at most one concise thirst signal for the week.

## Sunday closeout

Before the week ends, review unresolved tasks for either verified completion or an exact human handoff. Do not convert eligibility into an award; the reviewer still makes the final call.
