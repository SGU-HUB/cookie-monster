# Awarding

The designated human reviewer has sole authority over cookies.

The reviewer may:

- award one cookie for an eligible task;
- deny a cookie;
- defer the decision pending verification; or
- take back a previously awarded cookie for bad or misleading work.

The tracker only records explicit decisions. It must never:

- award a cookie automatically;
- treat task completion as an implied award;
- convert praise, silence, or acknowledgment into an award; or
- override a denial or take-back.

## Decision record

For each decision, record:

- task label;
- week;
- decision: awarded, denied, deferred, or revoked;
- decision source;
- short reason; and
- timestamp.

A revocation reduces the current week’s net total only when the reviewer explicitly ties it to that week.
